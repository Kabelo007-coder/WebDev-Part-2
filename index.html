Kay's Clothing Co.

We want to be trusted uniform suppliers of choice for electrical trade businesses,
known for combining safety compliance, comfort
and a professional brand image in the industry.

Kay's Clothing Co. was founded to solve problems for companies in need of uniforms.
The founder saw firsthand while electricians were working on residential and commercial
sites that the standard workwear was not built for the job. Off-the-shelf uniform tore easier
and lacked proper pocket sizes for tools(pliers and testers) and offered little protection against
flash skin and static risks. We started with small production of trousers and shirts for local
electrical contracting firms. The company grew into a dedicated supplier of uniforms and workwear
for electricians, now serving independent tradespeople and smaller contracting businesses.
